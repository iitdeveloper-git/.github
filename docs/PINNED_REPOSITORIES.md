## 🛠️ Implementation Steps for Organization Admins

To apply these pins on GitHub:
1. Navigate to `https://github.com/iitdeveloper-git`.
2. Click **Customize your pins** in the top-right section of the organization overview.
3. Select the 6 repositories: `demo_website_growixa`, `iam`, `ett_gns`, `iitdeveloper-website`, `iitdeveloper-git-shared-workflows`, and `pulsarip`.
4. Drag and reorder them into the sequence defined above.
5. Click **Save pins**.
